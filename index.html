Understand the AirAware terms.

AirAware uses practical atmospheric terms to help users understand how changing air may feel.

PLD

Practical Load Differential: a practical measure of changing atmospheric load based on pressure, air density, altitude behavior, and shifts over time.

DA

Density Altitude: a way to understand how the air behaves based on temperature, pressure, humidity, and elevation.

DA Burden

The difference between density altitude and actual elevation, helping show whether the air is behaving heavier or lighter than expected.

24-Hour Shift

A larger daily air-change indicator that reflects broader atmospheric movement.

30-Minute Shift

A short-term air-change indicator for fast-moving changes.

Environmental Score

A simple score that summarizes how current air conditions may feel.
